3. Political information and interpretation

Impuls is an independent project, unaffiliated with political parties, governments or public authorities. The app does not organise official votes or make voting recommendations.

Values profiles and proximity indicators use an editorial model. They support personal reflection and are not a scientific diagnosis. Community results describe voluntary participants and are not representative of the population. Ratings of political figures summarise user opinions.

Editorial drafts in the reading area are labelled as such. Sources, the dates of source information and explanations accompany the relevant content. Coverage and sources vary by country; a complete historical archive is not offered.

4. Target audience, voluntary information and privacy

Impuls is aimed at people aged 16 and over. If you are a minor, consent from your legal representative is required where the law applicable to you requires it.

Political answers are voluntary. Introductory questions can be skipped, and the reading and information sections can be used without providing your own political answers. An account is optional.

The app requires your explicit consent before processing political information. Private backup and usage analytics have separate consent choices; signing in alone does not enable private backup. These terms do not replace consent to data processing.

The privacy policy explains data processing, withdrawal of consent and deletion options.
