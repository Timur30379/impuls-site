Privacy policy

Updated: 22 October 2026

1. Controller and contact

Timur Felix Cön, Zaunhofstraße 30, 50997 Köln, Germany. Privacy and deletion requests: privacy@impulsapp.eu. Impuls is an independent private project and is not affiliated with political parties, governments or public authorities.

2. Political answers and consent

Impuls processes political answers and derives a personal values profile. Political opinions are sensitive personal data. We request explicit consent before this processing (GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)). Use of the app or sign-in alone does not grant consent. The profile is based on an editorial model, is not voting advice and makes no decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Local data and community participation

Answers, your values profile, settings and optional personal details are stored locally. In the normal app mode, community participation that you have authorised sends answers, question/country references and timing information to the backend with device or participation identifiers. A participation anchor and permission records support updates, protection and deletion. Answers and derived attributes can be linked under the same identifier. Pseudonyms remain personal data: replacing a name does not automatically make answers anonymous. You do not need an account to answer questions. This does not guarantee that a profile could never be associated with a person.

4. Optional demographic details

Optional age, birth year, postal code or region details are used locally in this version and are not included in answer backups. Optional server-side demographic analysis is not active in the current production version. No age bands or regions are currently stored in the backend for this purpose. Exact postal code, birth year and GPS location are not sent for this analysis. Public results are not representative.

5. Optional account, backup and recovery

Email sign-in or Sign in with Apple processes your email or Apple relay address, account/provider identifier and necessary session information. Your name and telephone number are not needed. Name metadata may remain from earlier Apple sign-ins; the current sign-in flow does not request a name.
Private backup requires additional explicit permission. It contains answers and profile data linked to your account. Once enabled, it may synchronise on later changes and sign-ins; signing in alone does not enable it. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, but is not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised server processes and sufficiently privileged administrators can technically read its contents. A deliberately established recovery link may connect an account and participation identifier. We therefore do not promise an absolute separation of all account and participation data. An Apple refresh token may be stored encrypted only on the server for revoking Apple sign-in authorisation; it is used only for revocation and removed afterwards. Account functions rely on GDPR Article 6(1)(b); political backups additionally require explicit consent.

6. Usage analytics and diagnostics

Only after separate consent, PostHog (EU cloud) receives usage events, such as areas opened, with a random analytics identifier and technical app/device information. These are pseudonymous usage data. The app does not send political answer contents or identify you using the participation or account identifier. Automatic screen recording and IP geolocation are disabled. You can withdraw consent under Legal & Trust, stopping further analytics transmissions on the device.
When configured, Sentry receives technical error reports even without analytics consent, based on the legitimate interest in stability and security (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Performance measurement instead requires analytics consent and uses sampling. Reports may include error/stack information, app version, operating system, device characteristics and technical events. Error and performance reports can be linked to each other through a persistent Sentry installation identifier. The app removes answer contents, contact details, credentials and URL parameters before transmission. Screen recording is disabled. Every network connection necessarily exposes an IP address to its recipient; we do not promise that IP addresses are never processed.

7. Providers and transfers

Supabase processes account, backup and participation data; the main project uses the EU region in Frankfurt. PostHog and Sentry use EU endpoints. EU storage does not automatically exclude access or subprocessors outside the EEA. Required international transfers depend on applicable safeguards, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about providers and safeguards.
Expo provides app-update delivery and technical distribution services. Apple and Google process data as part of their platforms, and Apple also handles Apple sign-in. External sources are contacted through the browser only when opened and are then subject to the provider's privacy information. Website hosting processes necessary connection data; the language preference is stored locally in your browser.

8. Withdrawal, deletion and limits

Community, demographic, backup and analytics permissions serve different purposes and can be managed separately. Withdrawal on the device stops the corresponding further transmissions even offline. This does not mean pending server cleanup is complete: retain the app data and displayed deletion request until completion is confirmed. Reinstalling does not reliably delete server data, and secure device storage may survive reinstallation.
Ending participation removes attributable participation data, including answers and shared demographics. Private account backup and local answers are separate. “Delete all my data” covers local data accessible on this device and attributable server data; “Delete account” additionally removes the authentication account. Account deletion includes the account, private backup and attributable recovery links. Pseudonyms from other devices that are not linked to the account cannot be discovered from an email address alone. Published, genuinely anonymised aggregate results may remain. A failed or partly completed step is not a complete deletion.
For privacy or deletion requests without the app, email privacy@impulsapp.eu. Requests are handled personally. We explain the next steps and establish the correct account before providing account-specific information or carrying out a deletion. Sending a request does not confirm deletion. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents or political answers.

9. Retention and response times

Local data remains until locally deleted. Account and private backup data remain until deletion or the end of their purpose. Participation data remains until deletion or the end of the collection purpose; withdrawal ends further use for the withdrawn purpose. Legally required evidence or limited security records may be retained separately for their specific purpose. To prevent delayed requests from recreating deleted data, a technical block record is retained: the SHA-256 hash of the retired random device identifier and the time it was blocked. It contains no answers or account link. The record has no fixed expiry while old requests can still arrive and is used solely to reject further writes under that identifier. Infrastructure backup copies expire through their respective rotation and must not reactivate deleted profiles. The deletion procedure provides information on the affected records and any remaining retention periods.
We handle deletion requests without undue delay and normally respond within one month of receipt. If a legally permitted extension is necessary, we explain its reason and length within that month. Proportionate additional information may be needed to identify the correct data safely. Acknowledging a request does not confirm deletion. For account deletions, a technical completion receipt without account identifiers or answer contents is kept for safe retries until the device acknowledges final completion, and for another 30 days afterwards. It uses a hash of a random deletion secret. This is not a general 30-day retention period for backups or other data. For separate data or backup deletions, the technical receipt contains a request identifier, a hash of the deletion scope and timestamps. Open requests are retained until resolved; completed receipts are removed at the next cleanup after 90 days.

10. Rights

Subject to the GDPR, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing. Withdrawal applies going forward and does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Contact privacy@impulsapp.eu. You may complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority.

11. Results

Comparisons concern participating profiles, not a representative population majority. Party profiles and values axes include editorial assessments.

In the app: Profile → Legal & Trust → Delete account. For further help, visit Support.

If you do not receive a reply, contact info@impulsapp.eu.
